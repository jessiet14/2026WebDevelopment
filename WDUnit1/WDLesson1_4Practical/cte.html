Computer Programming and Web Design

The computer programming and web design CTE is a program where students are able to explore the world of computer programming, web design, and video game development. They are also exposed to both concepts and practices in order to master the language of coding. Each course of this CTE has components of Project Based Learning in order to provide students tangible artifacts to demonstrate their passion, creativity, and technical skills. This CTE offers benefits that including engaging curriculum that fosters high graduation rates, work based experiences to bolster student resumes, practical knowledge readily useable in entry level positions or pursuant of advance educaation, workforce skills applicable to any career field, and confidence to innovate and affect the world among us.
